Inventory sources

Systems come from Ansible inventories and from inventory providers (hypervisors, clouds, clusters). All of them are re-read before every scheduled scan. Providers are set under inventory_providers: in config.yml, with secrets in environment variables (*_env) or files (*_file).

Providers

TypeFinds
proxmoxQEMU VMs and LXC containers of a Proxmox VE cluster, optionally the PVE nodes
vsphereVMs of a vCenter (REST API, VMware Tools for IP and OS family)
libvirt / kvmKVM domains of libvirt hosts, local or qemu+ssh://, optionally the hypervisor
openstackNova servers of a project (Keystone v3, application credential or password)
kubernetes / openshiftCluster nodes; their running containers are found with crictl and scanned per image
awsEC2 instances of one or more regions
azureVMs of your subscriptions (Resource Graph; service principal or managed identity)
gcpCompute Engine instances of your projects (service account key or the GCE metadata server)
hetznerHetzner Cloud servers of a project
DigitalOcean, OVH, Scaleway, …Ansible inventory plugins listed under inventories:

Windows systems (any provider puts them in the windows group) are inventoried over OpenSSH: build, installed updates and programs. CVE matching against Microsoft's security updates isn't built yet.
